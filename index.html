DUAL-ROLE · FEATURED

CitiFix

A Flutter app that connects citizens with city maintenance teams. Citizens report issues with photos and location; workers receive, navigate to and resolve them, then export a PDF report.

  • Citizen and worker experiences from one codebase, routed by an AppRole switch
  • Auth with OTP verification, tokens in secure storage
  • Map-based location picking and navigation to the issue site
  • Push notifications via FCM and PDF generation from completed tasks
  • Arabic / English localization
